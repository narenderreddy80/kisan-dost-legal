Delete Account / Data

You can delete your account in the app: Profile > Delete account. Your account is locked immediately. To restore it, email info@mitteeseeds.com within 30 days. After 30 days your data is erased and cannot be recovered.

You can also submit a request below. We will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.

What gets deleted: Your profile, field data, crop records, chat history, and all personal information.
What is retained: Anonymized transaction records required by law (up to 3 years).
